# IEEE Writing Conventions

Use this reference for IEEE-style citations, equations, figure and table prose, and reviewer-facing technical clarity. Use `section-playbook.md` for section structure, `argument-and-flow-guide.md` for the argument and paragraph sequence, and `engineering-prose-guide.md` for engineering wording and claim strength. The target venue's current instructions override these general conventions.

## Citations

Use numbered citations in square brackets, following the venue's bibliography style. Attach each citation to the specific technical claim it supports rather than collecting unrelated sources at the end of a broad sentence. Name authors in prose when their identity matters to the comparison; otherwise let the technical claim lead. Verify that a cited paper supports the statement actually made.

## Figures and Tables

Make each display answer a reader question or support a manuscript claim. The surrounding prose should state the main comparison or pattern, then direct the reader to the relevant figure or table. Do not repeat every table cell or trace in text.

Use the venue's reference style, commonly `Fig. 1` and `Table I`. Captions should explain what is shown and supply the conditions, units, abbreviations, and visual keys needed to interpret the display. Keep claim strength within what the displayed data and analysis establish.

## Equations and Notation

Introduce an equation's purpose before displaying it and define variables near first use. Explain what the equation contributes to the method or argument rather than leaving a block of formulas uninterpreted. Use consistent notation across sections and displays. Follow the venue's equation-numbering style and check every in-text reference after compilation.

## Reviewer-Facing Check

At the requested scope, ask whether the reader can identify the technical distinction from prior work, reconstruct the method, understand the comparison and its conditions, connect major claims to evidence, and see the stated limits. A readable PDF, traceable revisions, and accurate response-letter locations matter when those artifacts are in scope. For final packaging, use `ieee-submission-checklist.md`.
